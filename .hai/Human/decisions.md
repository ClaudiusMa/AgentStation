## Decisions

- Date: 2026-10-25
- Decision: Agents are commuters. After a moment on the platform they go up to work and are gone before the train leaves. A station has one exit, big and running out past the platform's end like a real one: a lift tower for three or fewer, an escalator for more. The project's name hangs over it as a big floating sign with a 3D position that scales with the figure.
- Why: The user did not want agents who get off to just disappear before the train leaves; they should go to work the way people take the train to the office. The user then chose one exit per station rather than both, a larger exit that extends out of the platform, and a bigger name that may float as long as it is placed and scaled in 3D.
- Tradeoffs: The sign on posts in the middle of the platform was removed; the station is named by the wall and the floating sign. The train waits (up to 40 seconds) until everyone has gone up, so a crowded station holds the train longer.
- Follow-up: Not stated.
